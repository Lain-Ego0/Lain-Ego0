### 🚀 About Me

I'm **Lain-Ego**, a robotics motion-control engineer and open-source community builder.

I currently work as an early-stage motion-control member at **Questech**, contributing to its second- and third-generation humanoid robots. My work covers motion retargeting, real-time whole-body teleoperation, hierarchical reinforcement-learning policies, and robot perception preprocessing. Previously, I led locomotion deployment for an open-source quadruped at **HighTorque Robotics**, taking policies from simulation to real hardware.

My core interest is making **motion policies run reliably on real robots**. I work across mechanical design, embedded systems, simulation, reinforcement learning, and full-stack deployment for humanoids, quadrupeds, wheeled-legged robots, and exoskeletons.

### 🔭 Current Work

- **Humanoid Motion Control @ Questech** — motion retargeting, mocap data pipelines, real-time whole-body teleoperation, hierarchical policies, and URDF-based self-occlusion masks
- **S-Dog Wheeled-Legged Robot** — a 16-DoF, ~13 kg platform using curriculum learning and reinforcement learning; demonstrated 14 cm obstacle traversal, 40 cm platform climbing, and self-recovery; exhibited at WAIC 2026
- **[G-Exo Active Lower-Limb Exoskeleton](https://github.com/Lain-Ego0/G-Exo)** — consumer-oriented 4-DoF active exoskeleton for outdoor hiking; mechanical, electrical, URDF, and learning-control development

### 📸 Selected Open-Source Projects

- **[HighTorque MiniDog](https://github.com/Lain-Ego0/HighTorque-MiniDog)** — open-source quadruped locomotion stack based on HIMLoco, with sim-to-real deployment, DreamWaQ/AMP reproduction, staged reward strategies, and reproducible documentation. `Reinforcement Learning` `Sim2Real` `Quadruped`

- **[ROBOCON BRS Parallel Quadruped](https://github.com/Lain-Ego0/ROBOCON-BRS_robot)** — 8-DoF parallel quadruped covering mechanical design, kinematics, VMC locomotion, FAST-LIO mapping, and autonomous navigation. Winner of three National Second Prizes at ROBOCON 2025. `ROS` `C++` `VMC` `Navigation`

- **[Bionic Lobster ROV](https://github.com/Lain-Ego0/BionicLobster-ROV)** — multi-DoF underwater robot with controllable buoyancy, heterogeneous actuator integration, and vector-thrust omnidirectional control. Provincial Grand Prize; featured by Xinhua News Agency and Guangming Daily. `Embedded Systems` `Control` `Underwater Robotics`

- **[OpenDoge](https://github.com/OpenDogeRobotics/OpenDoge_origin)** — crowdfunded open-source quadruped project with a complete simulation-to-real workflow and hands-on learning resources. `Open Hardware` `Quadruped` `Community`

- **[LocoWiki](https://github.com/LocoWiki/LocoWiki)** — a structured robotics knowledge base covering competitions, papers, open-source projects, simulation, deployment notes, and engineering learning paths. `Knowledge Base` `Documentation` `Community`
